# Bangalore Zomato EDA — 8 Key Findings

A data-driven synthesis of restaurant market structure, pricing economics, cuisine distribution, and consumer satisfaction across Bangalore, based on 8,921 validated establishments.

---

1. **Tech Corridors Drive Restaurant Density:** Electronic City (673), Marathahalli (484), and HSR (457) form Bangalore's largest culinary concentrations, powered by dense IT corporate corridors and student rental clusters.

2. **Heritage Neighborhoods Lead in Customer Satisfaction:** Traditional south and central Bangalore neighborhoods maintain the highest average customer ratings, led by Basavanagudi (4.05) and Frazer Town (4.03), outperforming newer peripheral suburbs.

3. **Mass-Market Dominance Under ₹500:** 86.7% of all restaurants operate in Budget (<= ₹200) and Affordable (₹201-₹500) tiers, with only 3.5% catering to luxury Fine Dining (> ₹1,000).

4. **Dine-in Real Estate Overhead Doubles Meal Cost:** Restaurants offering physical indoor seating have an average meal cost of ₹519 for two, more than 2.6 times the ₹198 average for delivery and takeaway-only cloud kitchens.

5. **Multi-Cuisine Splitting Unmasks Hidden Demand:** Analyzing cuisines without exploding comma-separated strings distorts reality; exploding rows reveals Beverages (2,828) and Chinese (2,764) as ubiquitous secondary offerings alongside North Indian (3,248) and Biryani (1,987).

6. **Non-Vegetarian Dining Overwhelmingly Dominates:** 92.8% of Bangalore's food establishments serve non-vegetarian cuisine, leaving pure vegetarian eateries as a dedicated 7.2% niche concentrated in heritage cultural zones.

7. **"The Sweet Spot" Decouples Price from Culinary Quality:** 765 budget-friendly restaurants (<= ₹250 for two) attain elite delivery ratings of 4.2 or higher, proving that exceptional taste and customer loyalty thrive independently of luxury pricing.

8. **Extreme Skewness Exposes Flaws of the Arithmetic Mean:** Review counts exhibit severe positive skewness with arithmetic means exceeding medians by up to 600%, driven by iconic viral restaurants with over 50,000 reviews that distort simple averages.
